Separate causes

The shared parser reads month first for day-first tenants. Correct its input contract and check that bookings and invitations preserve the intended date.

The booking-v3 form tells day-first tenants to type MM/DD/YYYY but reads their input day first. Correct the hint and validate it against the form’s parser. This required a separate ticket. The desk subsequently retired the form instead of fixing its hint.

Reports described as one-day moves remain footnoted under the computed cause. That description alone does not prove a timezone defect.

Arrival is not triage

Received and merged timestamps measure different events. A batch of merges is not evidence of a simultaneous incident onset. Use report receipt time to assess when customers first experienced symptoms.

Replace subject-only merging

Decision 0001 is an accepted historical record and remains unchanged. Its subject-only rule can combine different causes and split reports of the same cause. Propose a replacement decision: match tenant context, input path and cause; retain separate tickets for separate fixes. Unknown tenants need investigation before merging.
